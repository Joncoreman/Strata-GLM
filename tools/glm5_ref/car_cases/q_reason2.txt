A bag has 5 red, 4 blue and 3 green balls. Three balls are drawn at random without replacement.
(1) What is the probability that all three balls are different colours?
(2) What is the probability that at least two of them are the same colour?
Show the full reasoning with every fraction simplified. Then check both answers again by a completely different method (for example, drawing the balls one at a time and multiplying the probabilities), and say whether the two methods agree.
